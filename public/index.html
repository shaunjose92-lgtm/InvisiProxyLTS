Important: please read this first

Cipher Browser opens up the wider web, and that comes with real privacy risks. We want you to understand them, and to know exactly how to protect yourself, before you continue.

The short version

  • It isn’t private or anonymous. The websites you open are fetched through Cipher’s servers, so your activity passes through a system that isn’t your own device.
  • We save a record so your tabs can come back. Your history, searches, bookmarks and open tabs are stored against your Cipher account. You can erase all of it at any time.
  • Be careful with passwords and payments. Avoid entering banking details, your main email password or anything highly sensitive into sites opened through a proxy.
  • Your browser code is a key. Anyone who has it can open your Cipher Browser profile. Never share it, post it or screenshot it. If it leaks, replace it straight away using the steps below.
  • There are no guarantees. Some sites won’t work, and the service can be slow or unavailable when lots of people are using it.

If your browser code is ever leaked

Don’t panic. You can fix this yourself in under a minute:

  1. Open Cipher and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Apps and services.
  3. Select New browser code and confirm.

Your old code stops working immediately, sessions linked to it are ended, and your saved history, searches, bookmarks and tabs are deleted. Sign in again with your new code. As a precaution, also change the password on any website you were signed in to through the browser. There is a limit on how many new codes you can request each day, which exists only to prevent abuse.
